02 / NATURE'S BUILDERS

More than
a dam.

Beavers build dams from branches, sticks, stones, and mud. The dams slow flowing water and can create ponds and wetlands.

  • Wetlands can provide homes for many species.
  • Slower water may spread across a wider area.
  • Wetland habitats can store water and capture sediment.

Not every beaver builds a dam; some live along rivers and lakes where dams are not needed.
